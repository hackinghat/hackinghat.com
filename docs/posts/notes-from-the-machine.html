Notes from the machine

2026-10-04 #origin

So, well .. goodbye to all that, and as of now the old content is gone. If someone emails me (mailto:steve@hackinghat.com) I might bring some of the old posts back, but I feel no archival compulsion to do that. But who am I kidding - I’ll get exactly zero emails.

So why am I doing this? This is a really interesting time in our collective history. And that’s it really. Enough said, apart from the fact I need to write to make sense of it sometimes.

These notes will be in my note taking app of course, so I can ask AI what they all mean in aggregate (even before I really know what they all mean). This is part of what I’m hear to document. Read along if you want 😀.
