Notes from the machine

2026-10-04 #origin

Inspired by a post I read a few months ago about AI replacing SaaS I decided wordpress had to go. A github hosted self-publish site, is about $9 cheaper a month than AWS LightSail. So, well .. goodbye, the old content is gone. If someone emails me (steve@hackinghat.com) I might bring some of the old posts back, but I feel no archival compulsion to do that.

But, then why am I doing this? This is a really interesting time in our collective history. And that’s it really. Enough said, apart from the fact I need to write to make sense of it sometimes.

These notes will be in my note taking app of course, so I can ask AI what they all mean in aggregate (even before I really know what they all mean). But this is part of what I’m hear to document. Read along if you want 😀.
